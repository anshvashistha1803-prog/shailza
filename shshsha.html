Meri baby, meri cutie, meri favourite human. ❤️ Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon ki tum meri life mein aayi toh bas ek person banke thi, but dheere-dheere tum meri everyday life ka itna natural part ban gayi ki ab tumhe koi random baat batana bhi mere din ka favourite part lagta hai. Kuch funny hota hai toh tumhe batana hai, kuch irritating hota hai toh tumhe sunana hai, aur kabhi kuch bhi nahi hota tab bhi bas tumse baat karni hoti hai. Tumhare saath ordinary moments bhi mere liye special ban jaate hain.

Aur meri jaan, pichle kuch din se jo baat tumhare dil mein atki hui hai, main usse ignore karke aage nahi badhna chahta. Main yeh nahi chahta ki bas 2–3 din ho gaye toh main assume kar loon ki sab theek hai. Mujhe pata hai meri baat ne tumhe hurt kiya, and I'm genuinely sorry. Main apni past ki kisi galat cheez ko tumhare saamne example bana ke laaya hi kyun? Chahe main mazaak mein bol raha tha, mujhe apne words ka dhyaan rakhna chahiye tha—especially tumhare saath. Mera intention tumhe compare karna nahi tha, but I understand ki tumhe woh baat kaisi lagi hogi.

Main tumse yeh nahi keh raha ki us baat ko bas bhool jao, ya tumhe ab hurt feel nahi karna chahiye. Tumhari feelings koi switch nahi hain jo main ek sorry bolke off kar doon. Main bas chahta hoon ki tumhe dheere-dheere mere words aur actions dono se yeh feel ho ki tumhe kisi se compare hone ki, kisi se compete karne ki, ya apni jagah prove karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Tumhari jagah tumhari hai, Shailza. Tum kisi aur ki story ka replacement nahi ho; tum meri apni story ka woh part ho jiske saath main aage aur bohot saari memories banana chahta hoon.

I love all those things that make you you—tumhara care karna, tumhari random baatein, tumhara gussa, tumhari cute si zidd, tumhara hasna, tumhara mujhe kuch batane ke liye excited hona, aur woh little expressions jo shayad tum khud notice bhi nahi karti. Mujhe tumhara sirf happy version nahi chahiye. Agar tum upset ho, confused ho, ya tumhe reassurance chahiye, toh tum mere saamne woh bhi ho sakti ho. Main har baar perfect response nahi de paunga, but I want to listen properly instead of defending myself. I want to understand you better, not just win an argument.

Tumhari khushi mere liye important hai, but usse bhi zyada important yeh hai ki tum mere saath apni real feelings rakh sako. Tumhe mere liye strong banne ka natak nahi karna, aur na hi mujhe jaldi okay feel karane ki responsibility leni hai. You don't have to hide your hurt to make me feel better. Main apni galti accept kar sakta hoon, tumhari baat sun sakta hoon, aur apne actions se better karne ki koshish kar sakta hoon.

And baby, ek baat aur: tum mere liye sirf isliye special nahi ho kyunki main tumse pyaar karta hoon. Tumhari apni personality, tumhara dil, tumhara way of seeing things, tumhari little habits—yeh sab tumhe woh insaan banate hain jiske saath main rehna chahta hoon. Tumhari ek notification dekhke smile aa jaana, tumhare din ki fikr karna, tumne khana khaya ya nahi sochna, tumhari random stories sunna—even the tiniest things about you matter to me. Main tumhe pyaar sirf bade bade paragraphs mein nahi, normal days ke chhote actions mein bhi dikhana chahta hoon.

So meri pyaari girl, no pressure to instantly feel better, no pressure to reply in a certain way, aur no pressure to pretend that everything is perfect. Bas itna chahta hoon ki tumhe pata rahe: I love you, I value you, aur main tumhari feelings ko seriously leta hoon. Main tumhe sirf manaana nahi chahta; main tumhe better samajhna aur better pyaar karna chahta hoon.

You are my favourite person, Shailza. Meri comfort, meri partner in random nonsense, meri baby, aur woh insaan jiske saath main aage bhi chhoti-chhoti khushiyan collect karna chahta hoon. I love you more than I manage to say properly—and I hope I keep showing you that in ways you can actually feel. ❤️

Ek tight sa hug tumhare liye, ek forehead kiss, aur ek reminder ki tum mere dil mein bohot pyaari si jagah rakhti ho. Always. ♡
